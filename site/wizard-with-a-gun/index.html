Start modding Wizard with a Gun

  1. Download Mortar for Linux or Windows and open it.
  2. Pick Wizard with a Gun. Mortar installs BepInEx 5 and makes your first profile.
  3. Open Browse, add the mods you want, and press Play. Each profile keeps its own mods and settings.

Share a profile

A Wizard with a Gun profile shared from Mortar is a link to mortar.rethunk.tech/wizard-with-a-gun/p. Whoever opens it sees the mod list and imports it into Mortar in one click; the profile never leaves their browser.
