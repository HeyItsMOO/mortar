Start modding The Sims 4

  1. Download Mortar for Linux or Windows and open it.
  2. Pick The Sims 4. Mortar makes your first profile and keeps its mods apart from every other profile's.
  3. Open Browse, add the mods you want, and press Play. Each profile keeps its own mods and settings.

Share a profile

A The Sims 4 profile shared from Mortar is a link to mortar.rethunk.tech/sims4/p. Whoever opens it sees the mod list and imports it into Mortar in one click; the profile never leaves their browser.
