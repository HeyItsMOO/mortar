Mortar bot privacy policy

Effective 18 October 2026.

The Mortar bot is the Discord app Rethunk.Tech uses to run the Mortar Mod Manager Discord server. It is a private app: it is installed in that one server and nobody else can add it anywhere. This policy covers the bot only. Mortar's privacy policy covers the Mortar app, and Discord's privacy policy covers Discord itself.

What the bot reads, and why

WhatWhy
The server's settings, channels, categories, roles, permissions, onboarding, welcome screen, AutoMod rules and eventsTo keep the server set up the way its maintainers decided
Who is in the server: user IDs, names, roles and when members join or leaveTo welcome new members, give and remove roles, and carry out moderators' actions
Messages in the channels the bot can seeTo answer requests made to it and help moderators enforce the server's rules
Members' online statusDiscord sends it along with the member list; the bot does not use or keep it

Discord sends the bot this as the server is used. The bot acts on it as it arrives and does not keep it, except as described below.

What the bot keeps

The bot has no database of members or messages. The server's layout (the names and IDs of its channels, roles and rules, and the text of the bot's own posts) is kept in a private Rethunk.Tech repository so every change can be reviewed. A moderation action records the member's user ID and the reason in the server's audit log or its moderators' channel, which Discord keeps.

Sharing

Nothing the bot reads is sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train AI models. Discord processes it as the platform the server runs on.

Your choices

Leaving the server ends the bot's access to anything about you. To ask what the bot holds about you or to have it removed, write to legal@rethunk.tech. Audit log entries are Discord's records and follow Discord's retention.

Changes

A change to what the bot reads or keeps updates this page and its date before the bot starts doing it.
