Mortar bot privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026.

The Mortar bot is the Discord app Rethunk.Tech uses on the Mortar Mod Manager Discord server. It is installed in that one server; nobody else can add it. This policy covers the bot only. Mortar's privacy policy covers the app; Discord's covers Discord.

What the bot reads, and why

WhatWhy
The server's settings, channels, categories, roles, permissions, onboarding, welcome screen, AutoMod rules and eventsTo keep the server as its maintainers decided
Who is in the server: user IDs, usernames, display names, nicknames, roles, account age, and when members join or leaveWelcome, roles, moderator actions and the moderators' log
Messages in channels the bot can see, including edits and deletionsRequests to the bot, private tickets, and the moderators' log of edits and deletions
The server's audit logTo record which moderator changed a member's roles

The bot does not receive members' online status.

Discord sends this as the server is used. The bot acts on it as it arrives and does not keep it, except as below.

What the bot keeps

On Rethunk.Tech's own machine, the bot keeps a cache of recent message text (deleted after 30 days), members' current and previous names (deleted when they leave the server), and a list of the moderation actions taken through it. None of it is shared.

In staff-only channels, it keeps copies of edited and deleted messages and of messages a moderator writes a member up for, so moderators can see them after the original changes or disappears.

Server layout (channel, role and rule names and IDs, and the bot's own posts) lives in a private Rethunk.Tech repository for review.

Sharing

Nothing the bot reads is sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train AI models. Discord processes it as the platform.

Your choices

Leaving the server stops the bot reading anything new about you; what is already in the moderators' log stays unless you ask for it to be removed. To ask what it holds or to have it removed: legal@rethunk.tech. Audit log entries follow Discord's retention.

Changes

A change to what the bot reads or keeps updates this page and its date before the bot starts doing it.
